# Bad strategy

The idea is that a month of jumpy, uneven trading volume means informed traders were busy, so the next month is weaker.

![Calm volume does not keep the same sign](scorecard.png)

How one dollar grew. Grey is every name. Since 2005 the jumpy third finished ahead. Over the last ten years the calm third finished ahead.

![How one dollar grew](growth.png)

**Bad strategy.** It did not beat our benchmark, owning every name. Since 2005 the calm group turned one dollar into $43, against $68 from owning every name. It did beat the S&P 500, and that is not enough.
